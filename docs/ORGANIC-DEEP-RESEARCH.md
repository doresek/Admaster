# ORGANIC DEEP RESEARCH — SEO · Content · GEO, brain-driven (2026)

> **What this is.** Deep research (web-verified 2026-07, cited) + design that UPGRADES `docs/ORGANIC-MARKETING-SPEC.md`: it deepens Pillar 1 (FB page) and Pillar 3 (content engine), adds the missing **SEO/GEO layer** that Pillar 2 (website) must be built on, and proposes new tasks for `ORGANIC-TASKS.md`. Research basis: two parallel web-research sweeps (SEO/content · GEO/AI-answers), 60+ sources; the strongest are Google's own docs, Whitespark's 2026 Local Ranking Factors, Ahrefs/Semrush large-N studies, the Princeton GEO paper, BrightLocal surveys, and DataReportal Israel 2026. Third-party percentages are directional; primary docs are authoritative.
>
> **The thesis in one line:** search flipped from "rank the page" to "be the answer" — and the client's living brain (atoms with real prices, quotes, objections, local facts) is EXACTLY the raw material that AI answers reward and generic content lacks.

## 1. THE BRAIN→SEARCH ENGINE — how atoms become queries, topics, and answers

The core design: the customers-layer atoms ARE the keyword research. A pain atom is a search query in embryo; an objection atom is a "כמה עולה/האם כדאי" query; an unspoken_want atom is the long-tail question nobody else answers. This is what no generic tool has.

### 1.1 Atom→query mapping (the topic engine's core, upgrades P3-topic-engine)
| Atom | Query intents it generates | Content type |
|---|---|---|
| `pain` ("כואב לי ללכת") | symptom/problem queries ("כאב בכף הרגל בהליכה") | TOFU article + FAQ section |
| `objection` ("פחד שיכאב", "יקר") | "האם X כואב", "כמה עולה X ב[עיר]", "X מחיר" | MOFU comparison/pricing page — **commercial intent still gets clicks** (informational dies in AI Overviews) |
| `desire`/`aspiration` | outcome queries ("חיוך בטוח", "X לפני חתונה") | service page angle + proof gallery |
| `unspoken_want` | the questions people ask AI privately, not Google publicly — prime GEO material | answer-first FAQ/article |
| `alternative` (competitor atoms, C-09) | "X או Y", "ההבדל בין" | comparison content in the open lane |
| `proof` | "לפני אחרי", "המלצות", "ביקורות" | E-E-A-T assets |
| business atoms (prices, staff, hours, kosher/Shabbat) | — | the **information-gain injections** every page needs |

**Hebrew morphological expansion** (query variants: gender/number/construct forms, ב/ל/מ/ה prefixes) is applied at generation time — one atom → dozens of query forms ([Argos](https://content.argosmultilingual.com/hebrew-seo)). Priority rule: **commercial/transactional/local-action topics first** ("כמה עולה", "מומלץ ב", booking intents) — informational queries are 92–94% swallowed by AI answers ([Involve](https://www.involvedigital.com/insights/keyword-research-strategy-2026)); we still write informational content, but its KPI is *citation*, not clicks (§3).

### 1.2 Topic clusters from the atom graph (upgrades P3 + feeds P2 site architecture)
- **Pillar = service** (from `core_offer`/`real_solution` atoms) ↔ **clusters = the pains/objections/desires that atom-map to it**, bidirectional descriptive-anchor internal links. Cluster-structured sites weathered 2024–25 core updates better and earn AI citations at higher rates ([SEL](https://searchengineland.com/guide/topic-clusters), [LinkSurge](https://linksurge.jp/blog/en/topic-cluster-internal-linking-strategy/)).
- **Service×city pages** (`/service/city`): one per real combination, each with UNIQUE local proof (local photos, city-specific FAQ from atoms, local testimonials from the VoC quote bank). City-swap templating is now enforced "thin location pages" — including in AI surfaces. The atom graph is exactly what makes uniqueness scalable: each page pulls DIFFERENT quotes, prices, cases.
- Every generated page carries **information gain**: ≥3 business-specific facts (₪ prices, staff names, real timelines, original photos) — the operative quality bar post-HCU-absorption ([Hobo](https://www.hobo-web.co.uk/the-google-helpful-content-update-and-its-relevance-in-2026/)).

### 1.3 E-E-A-T from atoms (what a small business can actually do)
- **Author/practitioner entities**: the owner/staff as named authors with credentials, linked to GBP/LinkedIn/professional registries — person entities in the Knowledge Graph grew ~22× 2020–24 ([Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/blog/google-e-e-a-t-guidelines-an-overview/)). Source: brief + `persona`-adjacent business atoms; rendered as an authors module on the site (P2 requirement).
- **First-hand experience is the anti-AI-content differentiator**: real photos, case specifics, actual prices — atoms again. Health-adjacent clients (clinics) are YMYL-adjacent → practitioner-level signals mandatory.
- **DOJ-established reality**: engagement quality (Navboost) is a confirmed ranking mechanism ([Hobo](https://www.hobo-web.co.uk/google-vs-doj/)) — pages must be genuinely satisfying, which loops back to conversion craft (LANDING-PAGE-MASTERY doc).
- Disclosure: follow Google's stance — AI assistance disclosed where appropriate; AI-generated images carry IPTC labels ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content)).

## 2. LOCAL SEO — GBP as a first-class managed asset (NEW pillar-1 scope)

The single biggest gap in the current organic spec: **Google Business Profile management**. Whitespark 2026 puts GBP at 32% of pack ranking; Gemini grounds local answers directly on Maps data ("GBP is now an AI feed" — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/councils/forbesbusinesscouncil/2026/05/14/your-google-business-profile-is-now-an-ai-feed/), [Maps Grounding](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/maps-grounding)).

**Proposed capability — GBP Manager (`lib/gbp/`, new P1 task family):**
1. **Profile completeness engine**: audit + drive to 100% fields; narrowest primary category; **Services tab fully populated and mirroring the site's service pages verbatim** (jumped #81→#22 as a factor); accurate hours incl. Friday/ערב חג/Shabbat (open-at-search-time = top-5 factor; rankings dip before closing).
2. **GBP posting**: the P1 content calendar emits a GBP post variant per slot (1–2×/week + photos; active profiles ≈5× views). GBP joins FB/IG as a distribution target in the repurposing pipeline (§3.3).
3. **Review engine — policy-safe by design**: steady, un-gated, non-incentivized, non-scripted requests spread over time (the WhatsApp post-purchase flow we already designed is the perfect vehicle); auto-drafted owner responses ≤48h; target 4.5★+/20+ recent. The Feb–Apr 2026 policy tightening makes naive automation a liability — our autonomy-routed, rate-limited action model is the right chassis.
4. **API reality check (build-time)**: Google Business Profile API exists for posts/replies/attributes but access requires approval — flag as an external gate like Meta App Review; manual-assist mode (prepared post + deep link for the owner) is the dry-run equivalent.
5. **NAP/citations sync**: consistent name/address/phone across site schema = GBP = **d.co.il, zap.co.il, easy.co.il, מידרג, Waze** — citations fell to 7% of pack weight but are "the new backlinks" for AI-answer entity confidence ([Whitespark commentary](https://sambretzmann.com/2026-local-seo-ranking-factors-overview/)). Directory presence engine = a checklist/tracking capability, mostly owner-assisted actions.

## 3. GEO — the AI-answer layer (NEW capability family)

### 3.1 What the evidence says works (encode into every generator)
1. **Answer-first atomic chunks**: pages open with a 40–150-word self-contained Hebrew answer naming business+service+city; every H2 section survives extraction alone (passage-level RAG competition). Structure alone lifted citations +17.3% in a held-content study; query-matched headings 41% vs 29%; tables ~2.5× ([GEO-SFE](https://machinerelations.ai/research/content-structure-ai-citation-rates-2026)).
2. **Princeton GEO levers** (+30–40% visibility): statistics, quotations, cited sources, fluent authoritative prose ([arXiv 2311.09735](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735)). **The atoms supply all three**: real ₪ prices (statistics), VoC quote bank (quotations), review/press links (citations).
3. **Listicles own commercial answers**: present in 100% of "best X" and 71% of local answers; 92% of cited listicles carry the current year; self-ranking works; citations spread across 300+ domains — the long tail is winnable ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/)).
4. **Freshness cadence ≤13 weeks** with real ≥20–30% body change (new prices/reviews/FAQs) — date-bumps are detected and ignored.
5. **Simple Hebrew**: engines struggle with complex Hebrew morphology; short declarative sentences win ([Bidernet](https://bidernet.co.il/geo-2026-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%9C%D7%90-%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%93%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D-chatgpt-perplexity-gemini/)).
6. **Schema, right-sized**: LocalBusiness (narrowest subtype) + Review/Product **with populated concrete attributes** (helps low-authority domains most — [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6284518)); FAQ content stays as question-H2s (rich result dead, extraction value alive). Schema = entity disambiguation, not magic (Ahrefs null on generic JSON-LD).

### 3.2 Bot plumbing (P2 site requirement, trivial but mandatory)
Allow in robots.txt + verify server-side rendering (no JS-only content): `OAI-SearchBot`, `ChatGPT-User` (ChatGPT), `PerplexityBot`, `Perplexity-User`, `Claude-SearchBot`, `Claude-User` (Brave index), `Googlebot`, `Bingbot` (ChatGPT still leans Bing — **register every client site in Bing Webmaster Tools**, the forgotten lever). Optionally block `GPTBot` (training) per client preference. Skip llms.txt. ([OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots), [Seer](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/87-percent-of-searchgpt-citations-match-bings-top-results), [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/anthropic-claude-bots-470171))

### 3.3 Where ChatGPT gets LOCAL answers (act on this)
BrightLocal's 800-search study: business **websites 58%**, mentions 27% (Wikipedia 39% of those), directories 15% — with **niche vertical directories dominating whole verticals** (dental answers sourced exclusively from 10 dental directories) ([BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/uncovering-chatgpt-search-sources/)). → per-vertical directory mapping for IL (מידרג/דפי זהב/easy + the vertical's own directories) becomes a deliberate placement task per client. Hebrew citation behavior has **zero published research** — we should test first-party (§3.4) and own the knowledge.

### 3.4 GEO measurement — the prompt panel (NEW capability)
The Profound/Peec method, in Hebrew, per client: 25–100 local-intent Hebrew prompts ("רופא שיניים מומלץ בחיפה", "כמה עולה X ב[עיר]") run weekly across ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity → mention rate, rank-in-answer, cited sources → stored as `content_performance`-like rows → **the same diagnose→improve loop as campaigns**. Plus: GA4 "AI Traffic" channel (regex over chatgpt.com/gemini/perplexity/copilot/claude referrers — a floor, 35–70% arrives as Direct), and AI-crawler hits in server logs as the leading indicator ([Rankshift](https://www.rankshift.ai/blog/how-to-track-chatgpt-referrals-in-ga4/), [AuthorityTech](https://authoritytech.io/blog/ai-traffic-attribution-how-to-track-chatgpt-perplexity-gemini)). No tool does this well in Hebrew — both a moat and a sellable dashboard.

## 4. CONTENT ENGINE — calendar, repurposing, freshness (upgrades P3)

1. **One idea → many formats, atom-grounded**: pillar article → GBP post → FB/IG post + Reel script → WhatsApp broadcast snippet → email — the standard 2026 pipeline (40–50 assets from one pillar; [Newzenler](https://www.newzenler.com/blog/content-repurposing-system-creators-2026)), matching the spec's P3-distribution but adding **GBP as a mandatory output** and WhatsApp as the IL-#1 channel (88% usage).
2. **Calendar logic**: the P1 calendar generator's slot mix gets research-backed ratios — commercial/booking topics prioritized for click value; informational topics written answer-first and measured on citations; Israeli windows from `israeli-market-timing`.
3. **The freshness engine (NEW, heartbeat-native)**: a quarterly-or-faster refresh loop per page — regenerate with new atoms (prices, reviews, cases) achieving real ≥20–30% delta; refresh queue prioritized by GSC positions 5–30 + click-decay detection (20–30% drop over 4–8 weeks). **This is the moat applied**: the brain always has fresh material; hand-maintained competitor sites update yearly. Heartbeat monthly tick proposes the refresh queue; weekly executes ≤N refreshes.
4. **Measurement backbone**: per-client GSC property + API (Search Analytics 1,200 QPM/site; 2–3-day lag; 16-month retention) and **BigQuery bulk export** as the scale path ([Google limits](https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/limits)); track SERP-feature/AI-citation presence, not just rank (position 1 under an AIO can be invisible); geo-grid tracking for the map pack.

## 6. The honest risks
- **Zero-click means even perfect SEO sends fewer clicks every year** — the strategy hedges by (1) prioritizing commercial intents that still click, (2) winning citations (brand presence in answers), (3) owning direct channels (WhatsApp list — which our retention engine builds).
- **Third-party stats are directional** (AIO coverage 48–68% depending on study; freshness thresholds single-vendor) — encode as tunable constants, not gospel.
- **GBP API + directories involve external approvals/owner actions** — same external-gate discipline as Meta App Review.
- **Hebrew GEO is unproven ground** — that's the opportunity, but our prompt panel must validate what actually gets cited in Hebrew before we scale any single tactic.
