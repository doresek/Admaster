# PERFECT MARKETER — the master map (9 domains, gaps, unified roadmap)

> **What this is.** The integrating map of the Perfect-Marketer body — the complete world of a perfect marketer across 9 domains, AdMaster's current state vs gaps, extend-vs-build decisions, and ONE prioritized roadmap. The satellite docs carry the depth: `BRAIN-DEEPENING-SPEC` (domains 1–2) · `MARKET-INTELLIGENCE-SPEC` (the outward eye) · `MARKETING-TECHNIQUE-SYSTEM` (technique selection + judgment) · `ORGANIC-DEEP-RESEARCH` (SEO/GEO/content) · `WEBSITE-BUILDER-SPEC` (the operated site) · `LANDING-PAGE-MASTERY` (conversion) · plus the built stack (VISION-DEEP §8 capabilities C-01…C-11, heartbeat, autonomy modes, ORGANIC-MARKETING-SPEC pillars).
>
> **The through-line:** ONE system that looks INWARD (deep brain) + OUTWARD (market intelligence), encodes the legends + learns judgment from its own verdicts + stays current, and executes the full funnel — organic, website, landing pages, paid, retention — one brain, one trace, one learning loop.
>
> **The three biggest gaps, named up front:** (1) **MEASUREMENT** — attribution honesty, full-funnel tracking, unit economics (the heart; domain 8); (2) **CLOSING THE LEARNING LOOP LIVE** — the machinery is built, the live signals aren't flowing yet (domain 9); (3) **FULL LIFECYCLE** — acquisition is deep, conversion is specced, retention/growth is the thinnest layer (domain 7).

### D8. ANALYTICS & MEASUREMENT — the heart, and the biggest gap
**Have:** `content_performance` + live ingestion (T10, H4-gated) · verdicts/baselines design · C-03 calibration · C-04 shock (protects against misattributing market moves) · report shares. **Missing (the research-verified 2026 stack, none built):**
| Layer | What (research-grounded) | Status |
|---|---|---|
| L0 identity capture | fbclid/gclid/UTM/`lead_id`/`ctwa_clid` persisted onto every lead at entry — **attribution without stored IDs is guessing** | ❌ build first (S-M) |

| L4 unit economics | per-client: contribution margin → break-even ROAS = 1/CM · value-per-lead = close%×deal×margin · gates LTV:CAC ≥4:1 AND payback ≤6mo (services) — **feeding the allocator and every digest** | ❌ (M) |

**Doctrine (from the research):** MTA is dead — we do honest platform-attribution + CRM truth + periodic experiments (triangulation-lite), we LABEL attribution as click-based, we never show ROAS without margin, and MMM is explicitly out of scope below ~$100k/yr clients. `ctwa_clid` capture is mandatory for the Israeli WhatsApp-first funnel (natively, CTWA reporting is a black hole). Amendment 13: click IDs ARE personal data — consent + disclosure baked in.

## Honest limits (the map's disclaimers)
1. **Measurement ceilings are physics:** only ~30–60% of journeys are observable; we model the gap and label attribution honestly ("click-based") rather than pretend — the honesty is itself a differentiator vs tools that sell certainty.
2. **SMB statistics:** most clients will never support formal lift tests or per-client MMM; power-gated claims + fleet pooling + the "inconclusive" discipline are the mitigations, not solutions.
3. **External gates pace the plan:** Meta App Review (weeks–months), GBP allowlist, Basic Access, C2 — W0 exists precisely because these clocks dominate wall-time.
4. **Retention depends on owner participation** (lead marks, sales data) — the WhatsApp check-in + one-tap marks lower the friction; some clients still won't feed it.
5. **Taste, lateral creative leaps, relationships, crisis judgment** — VISION-DEEP §8.4 stands: complement with human input surfaces, don't claim.
6. **Third-party research numbers are directional** (flagged per-doc); primary docs (Google/Meta/law texts) are authoritative; our own fleet data replaces both over time — that's the point.
