# WhatsApp Marketing — the Israeli BOFU + retention engine

WhatsApp is where Israeli SMB deals actually close. It is a **permission-based conversation channel** — the moment a message feels like an ad, the reply is silence or "מי זה?". Every message must read like a competent human wrote it to THIS person, and every sequence is grounded in objection/desire atoms exactly like ads are.

## 0. The iron rules of the channel

1. **Speed beats copy.** A lead answered in <5 minutes converts multiples better than one answered in 3 hours; after 24h it's cold outreach. Response latency is a funnel node with an SLA — design ops around it before wordsmithing anything.
2. **Consent is real, not theoretical:** message people who initiated contact (clicked a WhatsApp CTA, left a lead form expecting contact) — and honor opt-out instantly and permanently ("הסר", "די", "לא מעוניין" → suppression list, no exceptions). Spam complaints kill the sender number; there is no recovery worth the risk.
3. **One message must never look like a newsletter.** Short lines, no banner images by default, no "שלום רב", no marketing-speak. The register is a helpful professional texting — dugri, warm, brief.
4. **Every sequence has an exit condition.** Replied → human/flow takes over, automation stops. No reply after the sequence → stop; do NOT keep pinging (the Israeli tolerance is ~2 unanswered follow-ups, then you're a nudnik and the brand pays).

## 1. The lead follow-up sequence (BOFU core)

The default 4-touch shape for an ad-generated lead; adapt spacing to the vertical's consideration time:

```
T+0 (instant)    Acknowledge + one qualifying question.
                 "היי {שם}, קיבלנו את הפנייה שלך לגבי {נושא} 🙂
                  כדי שנכוון בול — {שאלה מסננת אחת}?"
                 WHY: instant = catches them mid-intent; a question invites
                 a reply (conversation > broadcast); the answer qualifies.

T+2–4h (if silent)  Value nudge, zero pressure.
                 One useful thing: a relevant answer, a tip, a proof point
                 drawn from the TOP OBJECTION atom — pre-handle it unasked.
                 "הרבה שואלים אותנו על {ההתנגדות}. אז {תשובה קצרה}."

T+1 day          The concrete next step + friction removal.
                 "{שם}, אפשר לשריין לך {slot}? לוקח 2 דקות ובלי התחייבות."
                 The offer's risk-reversal component belongs HERE.

T+3 days (last)  Honest close of the loop + door left open.
                 "לא נציק 🙂 אם זה רלוונטי מתישהו — אנחנו כאן. בהצלחה!"
                 WHY: the graceful exit reads as confidence, protects the
                 brand, and — measurably — resurrects a chunk of leads.
```

- **Atom grounding:** the qualifying question comes from the sub_audience split (which segment is this?); the T+2h value nudge deploys the top objection atom; the T+1d step mirrors the offer's lowest-commitment component. A sequence that ignores the atoms is a generic drip — the thing we exist to beat.
- **Replies route to a human (or the owner) immediately** with full context; the system's job is to keep the pipeline warm and qualified, not to fake being human in a negotiation.

## 2. Retention & repeat-purchase flows (the LTV lever)

Acquisition is rented; the list is owned. For most Israeli SMBs a WhatsApp retention flow is the cheapest revenue they're not collecting:

- **Post-purchase sequence:** T+1d thank-you + expectation-set (reduces remorse/churn) → T+7–14d check-in ("איך היה? הכול בסדר?" — genuine, and it catches problems before they become 1★ reviews) → the review ask (§3) → vertical-timed repeat trigger (המשך טיפול, מלאי נגמר, תור הבא) with a one-tap booking step.
- **Reactivation (dormant customers):** one honest message, not a campaign: "עבר זמן 🙂 {הצעה קטנה רלוונטית}". Timing from the vertical's natural cycle (a salon = 5–6 שבועות; a dietician = אחרי החגים). Two silent reactivations → stop, mark dormant.
- **VIP/broadcast lists** (explicit opt-in only): early access, genuinely-useful content, chagim greetings WITHOUT a sales pitch attached (a greeting with a coupon is not a greeting — Israelis notice). Frequency ceiling ~2–4/month; every broadcast must survive the test "would a human forward this?"
- All sends respect **Shabbat/chagim quiet windows** and business-appropriate hours (default 09:00–20:00; never Friday evening–Saturday night for observant-audience clients — check the audience atoms).

## 3. The review-generation loop (feeds VoC + local reputation)

- Ask at the **peak-satisfaction moment** (vertical-dependent: right after the result, or at the T+7 check-in IF the reply was positive — never after silence or a complaint; route complaints to the owner instead).
- The ask is personal, small, and one-tap: "ממש נשמח אם תכתבו לנו כמה מילים בגוגל 🙏 {link}". A positive check-in reply first, ask second — sequencing doubles the hit rate.
- Every review generated feeds voc-mining (quotes → atoms → proof for ads) and the Google/Facebook local-reputation stack (competitor-analysis §5 — the recommendation-group economy runs on this).
- Negative check-in replies are GOLD: an intercepted complaint = a saved review + an objection/expectation atom + an owner alert. Log them.

## 4. The owner check-in (the interview a human marketer would do)

Monthly conversational check-in TO the owner via WhatsApp — the cheapest high-signal brain input we're not otherwise capturing:
> "היי {בעלים} 🙂 שאלה חודשית קצרה: איך היו הלידים החודש — רלוונטיים? משהו השתנה בעסק (מחירים/שירותים/עומס)? משהו שלקוחות אמרו לך שכדאי שנדע?"

- Parse replies into signals/atoms: lead-quality verdicts (→ audience atoms), business changes (→ business atoms + strategy re-sync triggers), customer quotes (→ VoC).
- Also the delivery channel for the weekly digest + one-tap approvals — the owner's whole relationship with the system can live in WhatsApp, which is exactly where Israeli SMB owners live.

## 5. Compliance & platform mechanics (InforU/WABA)

- **Business-initiated messages outside the 24h customer-service window require pre-approved templates** — design sequences so T+0/T+2h ride the open window (lead just contacted = window open) and later touches use approved templates with variables. Template approvals take time; author them with the atom variables planned ({שם}, {נושא}, {slot}) rather than baking copy in.
- A user reply RE-OPENS the 24h window (free-form allowed) — sequences should be designed to *earn replies* partly for this reason.
- Keep marketing/utility template categories honest (miscategorized marketing templates get rejected/flagged and hurt the number's standing).
- **Suppression list is global per client** (across sequences); sender-number warm-up applies to new numbers — ramp volume gradually.
- Message logs → `whatsapp_messages`; every send carries `grounded_in` like any artifact; reply/no-reply/opt-out are learning signals (a sequence's reply rate is a testable metric — creative-testing-discipline applies to message variants too).

## 6. Sequence-review checklist

- [ ] Entry = real consent; exits defined (reply / opt-out / sequence-end silence).
- [ ] T+0 latency ≤ 5 min; qualifying question present; replies route to human with context.
- [ ] Objection atom deployed in the value touch; offer's risk-reversal in the step touch; graceful last touch.
- [ ] ≤ 2 unanswered follow-ups after the first; quiet windows + hours respected (audience-atom aware).
- [ ] Templates approved for outside-window touches; suppression list enforced; number health OK.
- [ ] Reads like a human texting: short lines, dugri-warm, zero newsletter smell (read it aloud).
- [ ] Every message grounded_in atoms + logged; reply rates tracked as metrics.
