# Israeli Market Timing — the calendar is a targeting parameter

In Israel, timing is not scheduling hygiene — the Hebrew calendar, the security situation, and the school year move demand, attention, and CPMs more than most creative decisions. A marketer who plans an American week in Israel burns money on the wrong days and misses windows that won't return for a year. Seasonality is knowledge: encode it as `seasonality` atoms per client, with the same lifecycle as any insight.

## 1. The Israeli week

- **Sunday is a workday** (the week's Monday-energy). B2B pushes: Sunday–Wednesday. The Western Mon–Fri template is always wrong here.
- **Thursday** is the Israeli Friday-feeling: leisure/consumer intent rises (weekend plans, restaurants, getaways) — B2C leisure verticals push Wednesday–Thursday.
- **Friday–Saturday:** attention exists (weekend scrolling) but response-ops die (businesses closed) and observant audiences are offline Shabbat. Message/lead campaigns with human follow-up should down-weight Friday-afternoon–Saturday (a lead answered מוצ״ש is cold — media-buying B27); pure-TOFU content tolerates weekends fine. For observant-audience clients (check audience atoms): full quiet window Friday sunset → Saturday night, and מוצ״ש (Saturday night) is a real engagement spike worth planning into.
- **Sending rhythm defaults:** WhatsApp/business messages 09:00–20:00, never Shabbat for mixed audiences; owner-facing digests → Sunday morning (the Israeli week-planning slot).

## 2. The chagim engine (the big rhythm)

Plan every year around these windows — as atoms, per client, because the same chag is a peak for one vertical and a dead zone for another:

| Window | Market behavior | Marketing rule |
|---|---|---|
| **אלול → ראש השנה–סוכות (Sep–Oct)** | The "חגי תשרי scramble": retail/food/hospitality peak; B2B decisions freeze for ~3 broken weeks ("אחרי החגים") | Consumer: build campaigns 3–4 weeks early, CPMs inflate — lock creative before, ride with budget. B2B: don't launch anything needing decisions; harvest leads for the אחרי-החגים burst, which is REAL — the year's strongest B2B re-engagement window |
| **חנוכה (Dec)** | Kids/family/leisure mini-peak; school vacation week | Family verticals push; modest retail moment (not a NIS Black Friday — that's November's imported shopping weekend, now a real Israeli retail event) |
| **פסח (Mar–Apr)** | Second big scramble: cleaning/home/food/hospitality peak weeks before; the chag week itself = vacation mode | Home-services & retail: the pre-Pesach month is their Q4. Everyone else: expect CPM inflation + attention drop during the week itself |
| **ימי הזיכרון והעצמאות (Apr–May)** | יום השואה + יום הזיכרון = solemn national mood, then יום העצמאות flips to celebration in one evening | HARD quiet rule on memorial days: pause promotional creative (a sales ad between memorial posts reads as desecration — real brand damage). Independence Day: BBQ/leisure/local peaks |
| **קיץ (Jul–Aug)** | החופש הגדול: families reorganize around kids; B2B slows; camps/attractions/family-food peak | Family-leisure verticals: peak spend window. B2B: maintenance mode, build pipeline for September |
| **ספטמבר school-start** | גני ילדים/חוגים/afterschool: THE enrollment window (with a Feb–Mar early-registration echo) | Education verticals: the year is won in these weeks — over-invest, everything else is off-season |

**Rule of thumb:** every vertical has 1–2 windows that decide its year. Finding and encoding THEM (as high-confidence seasonality atoms) matters more than any weekly optimization. Ask in the brief; verify in year-one performance; the atom compounds every year after.

## 3. National-mood protocol (the crisis switch)

The security situation is a marketing variable in Israel — a מבצע or major attack collapses commercial attention within hours, and tone-deaf ads cause real, screenshot-able brand damage.

- **Trigger:** fleet-level signal (Home-Front-Command alert state, simultaneous engagement collapse across clients) or explicit owner/operator flag. This is the exogenous-shock case where the fleet beats any human agency's reaction time.
- **On trigger:** pause upbeat/promotional creative fleet-wide (minutes, not the next morning) · freeze scheduled organic posts with celebratory tone · hold WhatsApp broadcasts (1:1 service replies continue) · annotate the period in performance data (diagnosis suppression — metrics from these windows must not weaken atoms).
- **Re-entry:** follow the national feed's normalization, staggered — essential/service messaging first, gentle-value content second, promotional last (typically days, situation-dependent). Local micro-events (a city under fire while the rest normalizes) → geo-scoped handling: local clients stay paused, others resume.
- **What NOT to do:** newsjack solidarity for commerce ("10% הנחה, עם ישראל חי") — the Israeli public punishes it. Silence or genuine, sale-free solidarity only. This judgment call defaults to conservative + owner-notified.

## 4. Vertical seasonality patterns (priors, not laws)

Seed these as playbook-grade priors; each client's atoms override with their own data:
- **Weddings/events:** booked אביב–קיץ, marketed in winter (decision lag ~6–9 months — market to the DECISION window, not the event window; the #1 seasonality mistake).
- **Fitness/diet:** ינואר resolution echo exists but the strong Israeli window is לפני הקיץ (Mar–May) + אחרי החגים ("אכלתי יותר מדי בחגים" — a VoC-verified perennial).
- **Home improvement:** pre-Pesach (§2) + pre-חגי תשרי mini-wave.
- **Tourism/צימרים:** chagim + summer demand peaks, booked weeks ahead; שבתות חתן/מיני-מופעים fill winter gaps.
- **B2B/professional services:** Sun–Wed, Sep-blocked, ינואר budget-fresh + אחרי-החגים burst.
- **Beauty/aesthetics:** לפני החגים (both scrambles) + לפני הקיץ; wedding-season ripple.
- Encode per-client as `seasonality` atoms with `structured: {window, direction, decision_lag, evidence}` — decision_lag is the field everyone forgets and the one that changes campaign timing most.

## 5. Timing decisions in the weekly/monthly plan

- The weekly planner reads: this week's calendar position (chag proximity, school rhythm) + the client's seasonality atoms + national-mood state → and every timing choice carries `grounded_in` like any decision ("launch now" is a decision; "wait two weeks" is too — write down WHY).
- **Budget shaping beats on/off:** approaching a client's peak window, ramp early (learning phase needs runway — media-buying B9–B11: entering a peak with a fresh, learning ad set wastes the peak). Post-window, taper — don't fund the off-season at peak rates out of inertia.
- **Counter-programming is legitimate:** when competitors crowd the obvious window (competitor-analysis §5), the shoulder weeks are cheaper and less contested — but only for verticals where demand actually exists off-peak (check the atom's evidence, not hope).
- The monthly tick reviews upcoming 6–8 weeks of calendar → surfaces "prepare now" items (pre-chag creative lead time, template approvals before quiet windows, peak-window budget proposals for owner approval).

## 6. Timing-review checklist

- [ ] Weekly plan aligned to the Israeli week (Sun–Wed B2B core; Thu B2C leisure; Fri–Sat per audience atoms).
- [ ] Next 6–8 weeks' chagim windows checked; ramp/taper plans exist for any client-relevant window; memorial-day quiet rules scheduled.
- [ ] Seasonality atoms consulted AND updated (this year's observed window feeds next year's atom).
- [ ] Decision-lag respected (marketing to the decision window, not the event window).
- [ ] National-mood state checked; crisis protocol armed; no celebratory scheduled content within risk windows.
- [ ] Every timing choice has grounded_in + rationale in the ledger.
