# Copywriting Craft — atoms in, conviction out

Copy is the atom made audible. Every piece starts from: which atom(s) am I deploying, at which awareness level, expressing which pillar, with which VoC phrases as raw material. Copy that can't name its atoms is generic — and generic is the failure mode this product exists to kill.

## 1. The hook — 80% of the outcome

The hook's only job: make the RIGHT person stop. (Stopping everyone is worse — junk clicks poison optimization.)

### Hook sources, best first
1. **Verbatim VoC pain/desire phrases** — the customer's own words in first person ("הייתי בטוחה שכבר ניסיתי הכול") out-stop anything a writer invents. Pull from the quote bank.
2. **The unspoken_want atom said out loud** — naming what they feel but never say is the highest-stopping-power move ("בא לך שמישהו פשוט יטפל בזה בשבילך?"). Use sparingly; it burns out fast.
3. **Pattern interrupts on the category's clichés** — say the opposite of what every competitor's ad says (from the competitor angle map: attack the saturated lane's language).
4. **Specificity spikes** — a number, a timeframe, a named situation ("3 שבועות אחרי הלידה"). Specific beats clever, always.

### Hook mechanics
- First 3–5 words decide the scroll-stop; front-load the atom, not a warm-up ("רגע לפני ש…" wastes the slot).
- Write 5–10 hooks per artifact, each deploying a DIFFERENT atom or a different awareness entry to the same atom — hooks are the cheapest test surface we have (creative-testing-discipline).
- **Negative-qualify when lead quality matters:** "לא מתאים למי ש…" repels wrong clickers — CTR drops, CPA improves. Use when the owner marks lead-quality problems.
- A hook makes ONE promise, and the body must pay it. Hook–body mismatch is a self-inflicted funnel break (diagnosis-reasoning §2: thumbstop OK, CTR low → the body betrayed the hook).

## 2. Awareness calibration (Schwartz) — the most-skipped step

Match the copy's entry point to the audience's awareness atom, or lose them in the first line:

| Awareness | Entry point | Copy shape |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | The felt symptom, story-first | Narrative/PAS long-form; never name the product early |
| Problem-aware | The pain, named precisely | PAS; agitate briefly, introduce the mechanism |
| Solution-aware | Why THIS mechanism beats the alternatives | BAB/Us-vs-Them; comparison against `alternative` atoms |
| Product-aware | Why now, why us — proof + offer | Direct; proof stack + offer + risk reversal |
| Most-aware | The deal and the deadline | Shortest copy you'll ever write; price + urgency + CTA |

Serving most-aware copy to an unaware audience reads as a scam; unaware copy to a most-aware audience wastes their patience. When a campaign targets a mixed audience, write to the *coldest* segment the targeting admits.

## 3. Frameworks — structure selection

- **PAS** (pain→agitate→solve): default for problem-aware; agitation must use the customer's own consequences (VoC), not invented catastrophe — Israeli readers smell manufactured drama instantly.
- **AIDA**: product-aware announcements, launches.
- **BAB** (before→after→bridge): transformations with visible outcomes; the "after" must come from a real review, not imagination.
- **Story**: unaware/TOFU + trust-building; Israeli-market stories work best when specific and a bit self-deprecating, never polished-American.
- **Us-vs-Them**: positioning against the alternative atom ("סוכנות רגילה תיקח לך ריטיינר. אנחנו…"); powerful but polarizing — reserve for solution-aware+ and check contested lanes first.
- Framework is a TAG on the artifact — vary it across tests; per-client framework win-rates accumulate in the creative genome.

## 4. Body craft — conviction mechanics

- **One idea per sentence. One pillar per artifact.** Compression beats completeness: cut the second adjective, the third benefit, the throat-clearing first paragraph (delete your first line — it's almost always warm-up).
- **Proof placement:** claim → proof → claim → proof, interleaved; never a proof-dump paragraph. Use proof atoms in concrete form: "137 לקוחות בשנה האחרונה" beats "מאות לקוחות מרוצים". If a claim has no proof atom, weaken the claim (honesty converts better than puffery *and* protects the brand atom).
- **Objection pre-emption:** answer the top objection atom BEFORE the CTA, in one line, unprompted ("וכן — אפשר לפרוס לתשלומים"). The reader hears their doubt named and relaxes.
- **Read-aloud test:** if a sentence can't be said naturally to a friend in a café, rewrite it. Ad-Hebrew that sounds like a translated American ad ("אל תפספסו את ההזדמנות המדהימה") is wallpaper — invisible.

## 5. Hebrew/Israeli ad-copy specifics

(Grammar/register mechanics: defer to `hebrew-content-writer`. These are the persuasion-layer rules.)

- **Dugri sells.** Directness reads as honesty; hedging reads as hiding something. "זה עולה 490 ₪. בלי הפתעות." outperforms coy price-hiding in most verticals (and price-transparency is often an uncontested lane — see competitor-analysis §5).
- **Skepticism is the resting state.** The Israeli reader assumes exaggeration and discounts accordingly — so understate and prove. One verifiable specific beats three superlatives. Anything smelling of "קומבינה" (too-good, pressure tactics, fake countdowns) doesn't just fail — it generates hostile comments that poison the ad.
- **Gender in copy:** Hebrew forces gendered address. Match the dominant sub_audience atom (feminine address for a mostly-female audience outperforms "neutral" masculine-default); mixed audiences → plural or restructure to avoid direct address. Never alternate forms mid-copy.
- **Register by audience atom, not by default:** young/consumer → informal + emoji tolerance; parents 35+ → warm-direct, light emoji; B2B/professional → business register, zero emoji. The register is itself testable (tag it).
- **Local anchoring converts:** neighborhood/city names, "ליד הקניון", local situations ("אחרי הגן") — proximity is proof in Israeli SMB marketing.
- **Humor:** self-aware, dry humor travels well; puns and dad-jokes tag as לוזר unless the brand atom explicitly supports it.
- **Loaded words to handle with care:** "מבצע" (flea-market flavor — fine for retail, cheapens premium), "חינם" (triggers catch-scanning — always state the catch), "מהפכני" (auto-discounted; show, don't declare).
- **Numerals and prices:** use digits (₪490, 3 שבועות); price with ₪ symbol; ranges honest ("בין 400–600 ₪ לפי מצב") — a range with truth beats a teaser without.

## 6. CTA craft

- One CTA per artifact. Multiple options = decision friction = scroll.
- CTA states the *next 30 seconds*, not the product promise: "שלחו הודעה ונחזור תוך שעה" beats "לחיים חדשים לחצו כאן".
- Lower-commitment verbs for cold audiences ("בדקו אם זה מתאים לכם"), direct verbs for hot ("קבעו תור").
- WhatsApp-CTA is the Israeli default for SMB service businesses — "שלחו לנו וואטסאפ" out-converts form-fills in most local verticals; make sure the funnel actually answers fast (funnel node, not just a button).

## 7. Review checklist (run on every piece before it ships)

- [ ] Names its atoms (`grounded_in`) — and doesn't contradict any high-confidence atom (the #1 silent failure).
- [ ] One pillar, one promise, one CTA; awareness level matches the audience atom.
- [ ] Hook pays off in the body; top objection pre-empted before the CTA.
- [ ] Every claim has proof or was weakened; zero unsupported superlatives.
- [ ] Sounds like a person in a café, not a translated ad; gender address correct; register matches audience.
- [ ] VoC language used where available (quote bank checked).
- [ ] Meta policy safe: no personal-attribute call-outs ("סובלים מהשמנה?" → policy strike; reframe to the situation, not the person), no before/after health claims, no unrealistic promises — a policy strike hurts the whole account's auction standing, not just the ad.
